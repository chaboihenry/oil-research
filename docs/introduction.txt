Research Question: 
"How did the 2026 Strait of Hormuz closure change crude oil flows and import composition for the US and China relative to a fixed pre-crisis baseline, 
and how differently did the shock pass through to retail fuel prices in each country?"

On the March 23 adjustment, the NDRC's formula called for gasoline to rise 2,205 yuan/tonne but the actual increase was capped at 1,160 
roughly 0.85 yuan/litre less than the mechanism would have delivered. They intervened again in April, capping increases at 420 yuan/tonne 
against 800 under the standard mechanism

POLICY
  《石油价格管理办法》, issued under 发改价格〔2016〕64号, NDRC, 13 Jan 2016.
    https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xxgk/zcfb/tz/201601/t20160113_963566.html
    Art. 6 — crude bands: <=$40 priced as if $40; $40-80 normal processing margin;
      >$80 the refiner processing margin is progressively deducted, to zero margin;
      >=$130 prices in principle not raised or raised less, with fiscal measures.
    Art. 7 — adjustment every 10 working days, effective 24:00 on publication date;
      changes under 50 yuan/t are not made and are carried into the next cycle;
      under special circumstances (significant rise in the general price level, major
      emergency, or abnormal international oil price movement) the NDRC may, with
      State Council approval, suspend, delay, or narrow an adjustment, resuming
      normal rules once the circumstance ends.
    Note: the Art. 6 margin squeeze is inside NDRC's stated "implied" change, so the
    2026 wedge (2,205 -> 1,160 and 800 -> 420 yuan/t) is Art. 7 discretion only.

CAVEATS
  Throughput: ~15 mb/d is crude and condensate only; ~20.9 mb/d includes refined
    products. Both EIA 1H25. State which is used.
  NDRC basket weighting unpublished — implied changes taken as stated, not reproducible.
  No free source for China crude imports by origin; design is asymmetric by necessity.
  Origin is not route: Yanbu and Fujairah bypass Hormuz, so Gulf-origin is an upper bound.
  Aggregator sites used only to locate notices, never as data.


Steps. US first:

Align crude to the retail week — decide which crude days map to a Monday-stamped retail observation, and handle the holiday gaps
ADF tests on levels and differences — establishes which specification is legitimate rather than assumed
Distributed lag in differences, lags 0–8, estimated on pre-crisis data
Cumulative pass-through = sum of coefficients, converted to cents per gallon per dollar per barrel
Crisis period — re-estimate or interact with a dummy, and test whether pass-through changed
Brent and WTI both, reported as a pair

Then China:

Wedge summary — 24 baseline windows at zero, two interventions totalling 1,425 yuan/t
Convert to $/bbl equivalent for a comparable magnitude
Timing relative to the crude peak — when administered pricing binds
